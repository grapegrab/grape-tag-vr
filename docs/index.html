🎮 Arm Swing Movement

Natural Gorilla Tag-style locomotion using hand swinging gestures.

👥 Multiplayer Hangout

Connect with up to 20 friends in shared virtual spaces.

🎙️ Voice Chat

Talk to other players with built-in spatial audio communication.

✋ Hand Gestures

Express yourself with wave, thumbs up, pointing, and more gestures.

👤 Custom Avatars

Personalize your appearance with various avatar options.

🌍 Multiple Environments

Explore different themed hangout spaces and environments.

⚙️ System Requirements

  • Headset: Meta Quest 2, Quest 3, or Quest Pro
  • Storage: At least 500 MB free space
  • Internet: Stable WiFi or mobile connection for multiplayer
  • Hand Tracking: Optional but recommended for best experience

📱 Installation Instructions

  1. Download the APK file using the button above
  2. Enable Developer Mode on your Meta Quest headset (Settings → About → Tap Build Number 10 times)
  3. Connect your Quest to a computer via USB cable
  4. Open Command Prompt/Terminal and run: adb install GrapeTagVR-v1.0.apk
  5. Wait for installation to complete
  6. Launch the app from your Apps Library on the Quest
  7. Grant permissions for microphone and hand tracking
  8. Enjoy hanging out with friends!
